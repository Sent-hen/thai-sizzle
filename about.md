<div class="container narrow prose section" markdown="1">

Thai Sizzle brings the flavors of Thai street food to Mabank, Texas. Every plate is cooked to order: noodles charred in a hot wok, pork skewers grilled over high heat, and sauces made the way we grew up eating them.

We started out as a food truck so we could get to know our neighbors, and the response has been incredible. Thank you to everyone who has stopped by, called ahead or shared us with a friend.
